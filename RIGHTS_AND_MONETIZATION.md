# Rights, Legal Compliance & YouTube Monetization Policy
**Project**: HypeNova AI

---

## 1. Golden Principle: Discovery ≠ Authorization

A YouTube URL, channel upload, or public livestream is discovery metadata. **It does not grant blanket permission to download, clip, or republish third-party audiovisual material.**

HypeNova AI enforces strict rights classification:

```
[DISCOVERED]  ──►  Public reference metadata found via RSS/API.
      │
      ▼
[SOURCE_REQUIRED]  ──►  Original high-quality source file needed from authorized creator.
      │
      ▼
[RIGHTS_REVIEW]  ──►  Human/legal verification of license agreement or creator authorization.
      │
      ▼
[AUTHORIZED]  ──►  Creator-owned or explicitly licensed footage. Eligible for processing.
      │
      ▼
[PROCESSING]  ──►  Transcription, moment scoring, cutting, editing, and QA.
      │
      ▼
[READY_FOR_REVIEW]  ──►  Rendered 9:16 clip ready for executive human sign-off.
      │
      ▼
[APPROVED]  ──►  Scheduled to authorized channel queue.
      │
      ▼
[PUBLISHED]  ──►  Live on platform.
```

If rights evidence is missing, content remains blocked (`BLOCKED` or `SOURCE_REQUIRED`) and will **never** be auto-published.

## 2. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) & Reused Content Policy

YouTube rejects channels from monetization that merely re-upload third-party clips without substantial editorial transformation.

### Requirements for Monetization Eligibility:
1. **Meaningful Transformation**: Adding original commentary, insightful educational synthesis, narrative context, or creative parody.
2. **Setup, Main Event, and Payoff**: Clips must preserve the complete context rather than misleading, out-of-context splicing.
3. **No Automated Reposting of Unrelated Creators**: HypeNova is built for:
   - Creator-owned original gaming footage and podcasts.
   - Authorized creator clipping services with written client onboarding agreements.
4. **Advertiser-Friendliness**:
   - Severe profanity, hate speech, or sexual innuendo in the first 7 seconds triggers demonetization flags.
   - HypeNova automatically stars abusive words in subtitles and descriptions (`"F******"`, `"c******"`), preserving the audio while protecting metadata indexing.

The safest, fastest commercial monetization pathway is **Creator Clipping Services (Business B)**:
- Streamers and podcasters explicitly authorize HypeNova to edit and distribute their raw footage.
- Monthly Retainer Packages:

  - **Growth**: 30 clips / month (₹25,000)
