The Six-Stage Production Engine

From raw livestream or podcast footage to scheduled, high-retention vertical shorts.

01

Discover

HypeScout monitors authorized creator watchlists and RSS feeds with strict copyright and rights classification.

02

Analyze

Faster-whisper transcribes audio on CPU/GPU with millisecond word timestamps and voice activity cues.

03

Detect

HypeBrain's 7-factor viral engine identifies gaming ironies, roast punchlines, and contrarian podcast wisdom.

04

Cut & Style

HypeCut reframes to 9:16, centers speakers via YuNet face tracking, eliminates dead pauses, and burns animated subtitles.

05

Publish

HypeRank packages 5 title variants, descriptions, and hashtags before queuing via official YouTube OAuth.

06

Learn

Analytics Lab tracks genuine performance and format retention curves to continuously refine future clip selection.
